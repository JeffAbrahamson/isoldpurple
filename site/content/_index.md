  <p class="textContainer">
  The annals say: when the monks of Clonmacnoise<br>
  Were all at prayers inside the oratory<br>
  A ship appeared above them in the air.<br>
  <br>
  The anchor dragged along behind so deep<br>
  It hooked itself into the altar rails<br>
  And then, as the big hull rocked to a standstill,<br>
  <br>
  A crewman shinned and grappled down the rope<br>
  And struggled to release it. But in vain.<br>
  "This man can't bear our life here and will drown,"<br>
  <br>
  The abbot said, "unless we help him." So<br>
  They did, the freed ship sailed, and the man climbed back<br>
  Out of the marvellous as he had known it.<br>
  <br><br>
  <i>Seamus Heaney, Lightenings viii</i><br><br><br><br>
